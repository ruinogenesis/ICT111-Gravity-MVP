How Gravity works

  1. Verify once. Register with your RSU email plus a one-time in-person student ID check. We look, we never store your ID.
  2. Post or browse. One searchable place: categories, price filters, photos, campus pickup zones.
  3. Agree, then connect. Buyers request contact. If you accept, you both see each other's RSU email. Nothing before, nothing else, nobody else.
  4. Meet on campus. Apartment lobbies, the library, the student union. Busy places you both already know.

Trust and privacy, by design

We collect the minimum: a display name and your RSU email. Your email is shown to exactly one person, someone you agreed to connect with, and never appears on public pages. We never store ID photos or numbers, never ask for your phone or socials, and the demo runs entirely in your own browser with sample data. Gravity connects verified students. The meetup stays in your hands, so we recommend busy campus spots and bringing a friend for big items.
