RSU students: sell what you can't take. Find what you need.

The problem: students moving out lose time and money selling used furniture, appliances, monitors and clothes in scattered, unsearchable Facebook and LINE groups. Posts get buried and working items get thrown away.

Who it's for: verified Rangsit University students only, sellers moving out and buyers moving in. We keep it RSU-only so both sides always know they are dealing with a real, verified student.

Safer meetups, not promises: Gravity verifies students and keeps your contact private until both sides agree, but always meet in busy campus spots (library front, apartment lobbies) and bring a friend for big items. Gravity connects you. The meetup is yours.

Register: RSU students only

Every account is a real Rangsit University student, verified by RSU email plus a one-time in-person student ID check. We look at your ID once and we never store ID photos or numbers.

We collect only your display name and RSU email. Your email is shown to exactly one person, a buyer or seller you both agreed to connect with, and never appears on public pages.
