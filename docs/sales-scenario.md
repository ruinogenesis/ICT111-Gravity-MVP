# Sales Scenario — Gravity

## 1. Project Title
Gravity — RSU Student Secondhand Marketplace

## 2. Target User / Customer
- Primary user: RSU student sellers moving out under time pressure — graduating and international students who must clear a room in days (persona Su Su, segment CS01)
- Secondary user: RSU student buyers moving in — new and continuing students furnishing rooms cheaply (CS02)
- Decision-maker or admin role: Gravity team moderator — verifies accounts (one-time in-person ID check) and keeps listings clean (FR-09)

## 3. Situation / Context
It is two weeks before graduation. Su Su, a Year-4 international student, flies home on June 30 — her ticket is booked. Her dorm room contains a study desk (bought for 2,500 THB), a 45L mini fridge, an office chair, and a semester of textbooks. Last year her senior spent evenings posting the same items into three Facebook groups and a LINE dorm chat: posts were buried within hours, half the replies were "still available?" for items already gone, and the fridge ended up given to a junior for free. Su Su has five days of exams and no time for that.

## 4. Pain Point
Scattered, unsearchable channels (11/15 respondents, E01), severe time pressure — 30–90 minutes lost per item (E02), working items discarded or given away for nothing (7/15, E03), and discomfort sharing personal contact details with strangers (E05). Money is lost on one side while, the same week, incoming students two buildings away buy the identical items new.

## 5. Product Introduction
Su Su sees a Gravity QR poster in her dorm lobby while carrying boxes ("MOVING OUT? Don't bin it — list it."). She scans it, lands on the Gravity landing page, and reads the one line that matters to her: verified RSU students only, post once, contact stays private until both sides agree. She taps "Try the demo".

## 6. Value Message
For **RSU students moving out under time pressure** who experience **buried posts, wasted hours, and discarded items in scattered Facebook and LINE groups**, our **verified student-only marketplace Gravity** helps them **turn unwanted items into money within days, safely** by **letting them post once into a searchable, categorized market and revealing contact only after both sides agree**.

## 7. Sales Conversation / Pitch Moment
*At the dorm lobby table, Thiri (team) and Su Su (prospect):*

**Su Su:** "I have a desk, a fridge, and books. I fly in two weeks. Facebook groups are hopeless."
**Thiri:** "That's exactly why we built Gravity. You post each item once — photo, price, pickup zone like 'The Next 2 Apartment lobby' — and every RSU student browsing can search and filter it. No more reposting."
**Su Su:** "And I won't get random strangers messaging my personal account?"
**Thiri:** "No. Buyers press Request contact inside Gravity. You see the request, and only if you accept do you two see each other's RSU emails — nothing else, nobody else. Everyone on the platform is a verified RSU student — we check IDs in person once, and we never store them."
**Su Su:** "What if someone reserves my fridge and disappears?"
**Thiri:** "You control the status — mark it Reserved while you talk, flip it back to Available if they vanish, Sold when it's gone. Buyers always see the live status, so you stop getting messages for dead items."
**Su Su:** "Okay — show me."

## 8. Expected User Action
Try the demo from the landing page CTA → post a first listing → accept a contact request → tell one friend who is also moving out. For the course context: join our Lab 13 user-testing session and give feedback.
